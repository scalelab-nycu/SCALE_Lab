Course

Teaching Philosophy

Our courses connect digital IC fundamentals, memory-centric computing, low-power circuit design, 3D integration, and sustainable chip-design methodology. The goal is to help students build a cross-layer view from devices and circuits to memory subsystems, architectures, EDA flows, and emerging AI computing platforms.

Advanced Undergraduate / Graduate

VLSI & Digital IC Design

This course introduces the foundations and advanced practice of digital integrated circuit design, with emphasis on CMOS logic, timing, power, noise margin, standard-cell design, and design automation flows.

  • CMOS logic, delay, power, and robustness analysis
  • Standard-cell design and timing characterization concepts
  • Low-power design techniques and modern-node design constraints
  • SPICE / EDA-oriented design and verification practice
Graduate

Memory Circuit and System Design

This course covers memory circuits and memory subsystems, including SRAM, DRAM, nonvolatile memories, memory hierarchy, sensing circuits, peripheral circuits, and memory-centric computing concepts.

  • Memory array organization and peripheral circuit design
  • SRAM / DRAM / emerging-memory circuit fundamentals
  • Memory hierarchy and data-movement bottlenecks
  • Computation-in-memory and in-memory-search architectures
Graduate / New Module

Sustainable Design of Computing Chips and Architectures

This course module explores sustainable chip design from carbon-aware design concepts to energy-efficient circuits, chiplets, 3D heterogeneous integration, fault tolerance, and carbon-aware computing architectures.

  • Carbon-aware design methodology and manufacturing energy
  • Energy-efficient circuits and low-energy system design
  • Chiplet / 3DIC heterogeneous integration
  • Fault-tolerant & aging-aware design
  • AI-assisted and carbon-aware computing architecture optimization
Project / Seminar Style

Emerging AI and Memory-Centric Computing

This topic-oriented course discusses low-bit AI computing, green learning, hyperdimensional computing, digital CIM, emerging memory-based designs, 3D memory systems, and accelerator architecture trends.

  • Low-bit AI and energy-efficient accelerator design
  • Hyperdimensional computing and in-memory search
  • 3D-SRAM, 3D-DRAM and memory-centric interconnects
  • Research-paper reading and proposal-oriented projects
